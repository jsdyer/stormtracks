Inside the record

Tracks follow IBTrACS primary centres in the North Atlantic. The archive includes unnamed and post-tropical stages. Earlier records have sparser coverage. Short links between positions approximate the path, with breaks for gaps longer than 12 hours. Nearby results measure distance to the track centre. Wind, rain, surge and damage can extend far beyond it.

Winds use the US agency one-minute sustained estimate in knots, and pressure is in hPa (numerically equal to millibars). A dash marks an unreported value. IBTrACS may interpolate between agency fixes. The chart compares elapsed time from each track’s first included position.
